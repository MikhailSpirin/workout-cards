UPPER BODY · SIMPLIFIED

Simple Dumbbell Complex

60 minutes · 3 blocks · 12 movements total

45s work 15s rest 4 rounds
READY 0:45 Intervals completed: 0

Set up three weights

A · Medium/heavyPresses and rows
B · LightRaises and rear delts
C · Light/mediumArms and core

Choose each weight so you finish 45 seconds with about 2–3 clean reps left. Put the pairs beside you before starting.

0:00–5:00

Warm-up

No timer—keep moving.

  • Arm circles · 30 seconds each direction
  • Scapular push-ups · 10–15
  • Towel or band pull-aparts · 15–20
  • Light press and row · 10 each
BLOCK A · MEDIUM/HEAVY

Push + Pull

5:00–21:00

Four exercises × four rounds. Do them in order.

  1. Dumbbell floor pressFeet planted; elbows about 45°; pause lightly above the floor.
  2. Bent-over row, both armsHinge, keep a neutral back, and pull toward your lower ribs.
  3. Push-upsUse the dumbbells as handles, or elevate your hands if form fades.
  4. Single-arm row20 seconds per side; support the free hand and keep hips square.
21:00–22:00Rest · drink water · switch to the light pair
BLOCK B · LIGHT

Shoulders + Posture

22:00–38:00

Use the same light pair for the full block.

  1. Standing overhead pressRibs down; squeeze your glutes; do not lean backward.
  2. Lateral raiseLead with the elbows and stop near shoulder height.
  3. Rear delt flyHinge forward, keep soft elbows, and avoid shrugging.
  4. Farmer marchStand tall and march slowly without leaning side to side.
38:00–39:00Rest · switch to your arm weight
BLOCK C · LIGHT/MEDIUM

Arms + Core

39:00–55:00

Stay controlled; this is a finisher, not a max-effort block.

  1. Hammer curlPalms face inward; keep elbows beside your ribs.
  2. Overhead triceps extensionHold one dumbbell with both hands and keep elbows pointing forward.
  3. Close-grip floor pressKeep dumbbells close together and elbows near your torso.
  4. Dead bug with dumbbell pressPress one light dumbbell over your chest while alternating legs; keep your low back down.
55:00–60:00

Cool-down

  • Chest stretch · 45 seconds per side
  • Child’s pose with lat reach · 45 seconds per side
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch · 30 seconds per side
  • Wrist and forearm stretch · 30 seconds per side
Progression and safety notes

Run this workout at most twice weekly, with at least 48 hours between sessions.

First add clean reps within each 45-second interval. When all four rounds feel controlled, increase the dumbbell weight by the smallest available step.

If your back starts rounding or your shoulders lose position, reduce the weight instead of grinding through the interval. Stop for sharp or unusual pain.
