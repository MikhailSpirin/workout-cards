FULL BODY · MEDIUM INTENSITY

CrossFit 50

Exactly 50 minutes · barbell, dumbbell, kettlebell + box

50 minutes 6–7 effort / 10 5 phases
0:00–8:00 · WARM-UP

Two relaxed rounds

Move continuously at an easy pace.

  • 10 air squats
  • 8 alternating box step-ups
  • 10 kettlebell deadlifts
  • 8 empty-bar good mornings
  • 6 empty-bar front squats
  • 6 empty-bar strict presses
8:00–20:00 · BARBELL

Barbell complex

E2MOM × 6
5Deadlifts
4Hang power cleans
3Front squats
2Push presses

Complete each set without releasing the bar. Use a load you could perform for 2–3 more clean reps and finish each set in about 40–60 seconds.

20:00–24:00Walk, drink water, and test the conditioning weights.
24:00–42:00 · CONDITIONING

Three-minute EMOM

6 rounds
110 alternating dumbbell snatches Minute 1
212 kettlebell swings Minute 2
310 alternating box step-ups Minute 3

Each station should take about 30–40 seconds. Rest for the remainder of the minute. Hold the kettlebell goblet-style on the step-ups only if bodyweight feels easy.

42:00–50:00 · COOL-DOWN

Downshift

  • 2 minutes easy walking
  • 1 minute child’s pose
  • 1 minute hip-flexor stretch per side
  • 1 minute hamstring stretch per side
  • 1 minute front-rack or shoulder stretch
Intensity and scaling

Aim for RPE 6–7/10: steady breathing, clean technique, and no grinding reps.

Reduce the load, reps, or box height if a station takes longer than 40 seconds or your technique starts to deteriorate.
