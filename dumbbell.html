Dumbbells — Workout A

Full body · 10 exercises · approximately 60–80 minutes

Log format: reps × weight. Weight is per dumbbell unless the exercise uses one dumbbell.

1. Dumbbell Bench Press

3 sets × 8–15 reps · Rest 90–120 seconds

Last try: 12 reps × 25 lb each · medium effort

START
 END
  • Plant your feet and keep your upper back tight on the bench.
  • Lower the dumbbells under control until the handles reach chest height.
  • Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows as you press.

2. Dumbbell Pullover

3 sets × 8–15 reps · Rest 60–90 seconds

Last try: 15 reps · note: “2 × 70 lb pulls to chest” · medium effort

START
 END
  • Hold one dumbbell securely over your chest with both hands.
  • Keep a small, fixed bend in your elbows as you lower it behind your head.
  • Stop before your shoulders lose a comfortable, controlled position.

3. Dumbbell Overhead Press

3 sets × 8–15 reps · Rest 90–120 seconds

Last try: 12 reps × 12 lb each · medium effort

START
 END
  • Brace your abdomen and keep your ribs from flaring upward.
  • Press both dumbbells together without using leg drive.
  • Finish with the weights balanced over your shoulders.

4. Dumbbell Biceps Curl

3 sets × 12–20 reps · Rest 60–90 seconds

Last try: 12 reps × 15 lb each · medium effort

START
 END
  • Stand tall with your elbows close to your ribs.
  • Curl without swinging your torso or moving your elbows forward.
  • Lower slowly until your arms are nearly straight.

5. Overhead Dumbbell Triceps Extension

3 sets × 15–20 reps · Rest 60–90 seconds

Last try: 12 reps × 12 lb · medium effort

START
 END
  • Keep your upper arm vertical and your elbow pointing forward.
  • Move at the elbow while keeping your torso still.
  • Use one arm as pictured, or hold one dumbbell with both hands.

6. Dumbbell Rear Delt Fly

3 sets × 15–20 reps · Rest 45–75 seconds

Last try: 12 reps × 12 lb each · medium effort

START
 END
  • Support your chest and let the dumbbells hang below your shoulders.
  • Raise your arms out to the sides with softly bent elbows.
  • Stop around shoulder height and avoid shrugging.

7. Dumbbell Lateral Raise

3 sets × 15–20 reps · Rest 45–75 seconds

Last try: 12 reps × 12 lb each · medium effort

START
 END
  • Keep a soft bend in your elbows and your shoulders down.
  • Raise the weights out to the sides without swinging.
  • Stop near shoulder height, then lower under control.

8. Dumbbell Front Squat

3 sets × 8–15 reps · Rest 90–120 seconds

Last try: 20 lb each · reps not recorded · medium effort

START
 END
  • Hold the dumbbells at shoulder height and brace your abdomen.
  • Sit down between your hips while keeping your chest tall.
  • Keep your knees tracking in the same direction as your toes.

9. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

3 sets × 8–15 reps · Rest 90–120 seconds

Last try: 15 reps × 35 lb each · medium effort

START
 END
  • Begin tall with slightly bent knees and the weights close to your thighs.
  • Push your hips backward while keeping your spine neutral.
  • Stop when your hamstrings limit the hinge, then stand by driving your hips forward.

10. Dumbbell Calf Raise

3 sets × 15–25 reps · Rest 45–75 seconds

Last try: not recorded

START
 END
  • Stand tall with even pressure across the balls of both feet.
  • Rise as high as you can without rolling your ankles outward.
  • Pause briefly at the top and lower slowly.

How to progress

Keep the weight until all three sets reach the top of the target range with clean form and about two good reps still available. Then move to the next dumbbell weight and build the reps back up.

Before training: Warm up for 5–10 minutes and do a light practice set before the first press, squat, and hinge.

Safety: Use controlled repetitions and stop if you feel sharp or unusual pain. For pullovers and overhead extensions, choose a weight you can hold securely above your head.
