BACK · GRIP · CORE

Back + Core

35–45 minutes · 5 exercises

3–4 sets 5 exercises 1× weekly
Equipment Dumbbells + a pull-up bar or long resistance band
OPTIONAL SESSION

Workout

35–45 min
1Pull-ups 4 × 3–10 · rest 2 min2 pictures

No pull-up bar? Anchor a long band overhead, kneel, and pull your elbows down toward your ribs for 8–15 reps.

2One-arm dumbbell row 3 × 8–15 / side · rest 90s2 pictures
3Face pull 3 × 12–20 · rest 60s2 pictures

The picture uses a cable. A band anchored at eye level works too. Pull toward your face with elbows high.

4Farmer walk or march 3 × 30–45s · rest 60s2 pictures

Hold a heavy dumbbell in each hand. Walk, or march in place, while staying tall without leaning.

5Dead bug 3 × 8–12 / side · rest 60s2 pictures

Press your lower back into the floor. Slowly lower the opposite arm and leg, return, then switch sides.

Finish · 3–5 minutes Hang gently · stretch lats · slow breathing

Stop each set with 1–2 clean reps left. Stop for sharp pain.
