Upper-Body Barbell Workout

45–60 minutes · twice weekly · leave at least two days between sessions

1. Barbell Bench Press

4 sets × 6–8 reps · Rest 2–3 minutes

START
 END
  • Feet planted; upper back tight on the bench.
  • Lower to your lower-to-mid chest without bouncing.
  • Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows.

2. Bent-Over Barbell Row

4 sets × 6–10 reps · Rest 2–3 minutes

START
 END
  • Push your hips back and keep your spine neutral.
  • Pull the bar toward your lower ribs.
  • Keep the same torso angle for the whole set.

3. Standing Overhead Press

3 sets × 6–8 reps · Rest 2–3 minutes

START
 END
  • Brace your abdomen and squeeze your glutes.
  • Press without using leg drive or leaning back.
  • Finish with the bar over your shoulders and mid-foot.

4. Close-Grip Bench Press

3 sets × 8–10 reps · Rest 60–90 seconds

START
 END
  • Place your hands approximately shoulder-width apart.
  • Keep your elbows angled close to your torso.
  • Touch your lower chest gently; do not bounce.

5. Barbell Curl

3 sets × 10–12 reps · Rest 60–90 seconds

START
 END
  • Stand tall with your elbows beside your ribs.
  • Curl without swinging or leaning backward.
  • Lower the bar slowly until your arms are nearly straight.

6. Barbell Shrug

3 sets × 10–15 reps · Rest 60–90 seconds

START
 END
  • Stand tall and keep your arms straight.
  • Raise your shoulders straight toward your ears.
  • Pause, then lower. Do not roll your shoulders.

How to progress

Choose a weight that leaves about two clean reps available. When you reach the top of the rep range on every set, add 1–2.5 kg next time.

Safety: Warm up first, use rack safety arms for presses, and stop if you feel sharp or unusual pain.
